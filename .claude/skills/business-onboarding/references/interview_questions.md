# Interview question bank

Ask one at a time, in roughly this order, skipping what is already known. "Feeds" shows which guideline section the answer fills.

## Contents
1. Business basics  2. Positioning  3. Audience  4. Products and offers  5. Voice  6. Visual identity
7. Prohibited claims  8. Goals  9. Platforms and capacity  10. Competitors and inspiration

## 1. Business basics (feeds: brand_guideline)
1. In one or two sentences, what does your business do and for whom?
2. What is the business called, and is there a tagline you actually use?
3. How long have you been running, and how big is the team (just you, 2-5, more)?
4. Where do customers find you today (word of mouth, Instagram, walk-ins, ads)?

## 2. Positioning (feeds: positioning)
5. Why do customers pick you over the alternatives? Give me the real reason, not the brochure line.
6. What do you do that competitors do not, or do worse?
7. Complete the sentence: "We are the only ___ that ___."
8. What is one thing customers often misunderstand about you?

## 3. Audience (feeds: audience)
9. Describe your best customer: who are they, and what were they trying to solve when they found you?
10. What frustrates them before they buy? (their words if you remember them)
11. What do they worry about when deciding, and what finally convinces them?
12. Is there a second customer type worth planning content for?

## 4. Products, services and offers (feeds: products_services, offers)
13. What are your 3-5 main products or services, and roughly what do they cost?
14. Which one do you most want to sell more of, and why?
15. Do you have a standing offer, free trial, audit or lead magnet? What is the action you want people to take (DM, comment keyword, book, buy)?
16. Any seasonal or upcoming launches in the next 3 months?

## 5. Voice (feeds: brand_voice)
17. If your brand were a person at a party, how would they talk? Pick up to three: warm, playful, direct, expert, premium, rebellious, calm, something else.
18. Name a phrase you love to use and a phrase you would never use.
19. Show me a post or message you wrote that sounded exactly like you.
20. Do you use slang, local language, emojis, or a mix of languages? Which, and how much?

## 6. Visual identity (feeds: colors, fonts, artifact theme)
21. Do you have a logo, brand colours and fonts? Share files or hex codes; if not, what feeling should the visuals give?
22. Name 1-2 brands or accounts whose look you admire, and what you like about it.
23. Anything visual that is off limits (faces, certain colours, stock photos)?

## 7. Prohibited claims (feeds: prohibited_claims)
24. What must we never say or promise (guaranteed results, health or income claims, price promises, naming competitors, legal limits in your industry)?
25. Are there topics or events to stay away from (politics, religion, competitors' scandals)?

## 8. Goals (feeds: brand_guideline goals)
26. What should content achieve in the next 3 months: more followers, enquiries, bookings, sales, authority? If one, which matters most?
27. What number would make you say it worked (e.g. 20 enquiries a month)?

## 9. Platforms and capacity (feeds: brand_guideline platforms)
28. Which platforms matter, and which one is first priority?
29. How many posts per week can you realistically approve and publish? Who approves?
30. Can you appear on camera or provide raw footage and photos, or should content be mostly graphics and text?

## 10. Competitors and inspiration (feeds: competitors.md)
31. Who are 3-5 competitors or accounts in your space to study? (handles or links)
32. Who is one account outside your industry whose content you envy?

## Closing check
- "Anything important about the business I have not asked?"
- Read back the one-paragraph summary and positioning statement and ask for corrections.
