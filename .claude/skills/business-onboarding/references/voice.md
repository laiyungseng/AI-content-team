# Stage 2: 02-guidelines/voice-and-rules.md

How the brand sounds, how its visuals should be described, and the hard rules every post must pass.

This is a menu, not a template. The file gets a section only for what the user actually gave you, grouped and named to suit the brand.

Read `01-brand/brand.md` first: personality, values, and the customer's own words there are the raw material. Propose a starting voice straight from it: "your brand file says warm and no-nonsense, so I'd expect short sentences, plain words, no corporate filler. Right?"

**Ask for their writing on turn 1.** Two or three real things they have written: a caption, an email, a text to a customer. How they already write is their voice. Naming it beats inventing it.

Do not ask "what is your voice?" Show two short sample captions in different voices (the contrast pair in `examples.md`) and ask which sounds like them and what is wrong with the other. **Their edits are the signal, not their pick.**

## Core (needed before sign-off)

1. **Voice summary.** One sentence plus at least 2 traits as "we sound X, never Y", grounded in their real writing or their edits to your samples.
2. **On-brand examples.** At least 2 lines that nail the voice, each with why. Build from their real posts or their edits to your drafts. This is the most-used part of the file.
3. **Basic mechanics.** "We" or "I", emoji yes or no, and the language(s) they write in. Propose sensible defaults; most users just want a reasonable call made and confirmed.
4. **Overall visual look.** One sentence plus a few adjectives that go into every image prompt. If they have no visual identity, ask for one or two accounts whose look they like and propose a look from that plus their brand personality; an accepted proposal counts. Say up front that this folder never makes images, it only writes descriptions and prompts for them.
5. **Hard rules and legal limits.** Ask plainly: "is there anything you legally or personally must never say or show?" **Fact.** Always written: either the rules they confirm, or "None stated by the user as of YYYY-MM-DD." Record only what they confirm. Never guess a legal or safety rule.

## Topics to explore (raise each at least once)

### Voice and tone
- Tone by context: launch, educational, promo, trending topic, complaint reply. Only the contexts they actually post in. Write one sample line each and have them react; see the tone bank in `examples.md`.
- Vocabulary: signature words and phrases; words to avoid, each with a replacement. Mine their own speech and their customers' words from `brand.md`.
- Mechanics beyond the basics: sentence length, capitalisation, exclamation marks, ellipses, ALL CAPS, numbers as digits or words, how the reader is addressed.
- Humour: on-brand or not, what kind, what is off-limits.
- Off-brand examples: 1 or 2 lines that miss, with why.

### Visual direction (direction only)
- Logo usage notes.
- Colour palette, with hex codes if known, and the role of each colour. **Fact.**
- Typography: typefaces, weights, hierarchy. **Fact.**
- Photography and image style: lighting, colour grade, composition, rules on showing people, styles to avoid (these become negative prompts). Start from the visual look bank in `examples.md`.
- Reusable image-prompt building blocks.
- Video style.
- Whether AI-generated visuals are allowed.

### Rules and compliance
- Always do: non-negotiables per post (CTA, alt text, correct handles, disclosure where needed).
- Never do: absolute prohibitions (banned topics, competitor bashing, unapproved discounts).
- Legal and compliance: disclosure for sponsored or affiliate content, proof required for testimonials and results claims, pricing approval, UGC permission before reposting, copyright for music, images, fonts. **Fact.**
- Sensitive topics: never commented on, allowed only with owner sign-off, and the crisis rule (propose the default: pause scheduled posts until reviewed).

Account-wide banned **claims** live in the Banned claims section of `01-brand/brand.md`, not here. If one surfaces, hand it back to the brand file.

The pre-publish checklist lives in `06-content/workflow.md`, not in this file. Do not copy it here.
