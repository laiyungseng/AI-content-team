# Brutal Evaluation Rules

Be strict. Do not give high scores just because an idea sounds nice.

A strong ad angle must pass these questions:

1. Does it reveal a real customer pain?
2. Does it explain why the buyer does not buy?
3. Does it have strong contrast?
4. Does it feel specific to this industry?
5. Does it naturally connect to the offer?
6. Can it attract quality leads?
7. Can it avoid wrong-fit customers?
8. Can it become a strong visual ad?
9. Does it sound human and local?
10. Is it safe from misleading claims?

Score each category from 1 to 5:

| Category | Question |
|---|---|
| Pain depth | Does it touch a real pain or just a surface problem? |
| Market specificity | Does it feel specific to this industry and market? |
| Buyer hesitation | Does it address why the buyer does not buy? |
| Contrast | Is there a strong reframing moment? |
| Offer fit | Does it naturally connect to what is being sold? |
| Sales potential | Can this attract leads with buying intent? |
| Wrong-fit filtering | Does it avoid cheap or wrong-fit customers? |
| Creative potential | Can it become a strong video, image, or carousel? |
| Language sharpness | Does it sound human, local, and not AI-generated? |
| Compliance safety | Is the claim safe and not misleading? |

Total score: 50 points.

Rating:

- 45-50: Strong test candidate
- 40-44: Good, can test
- 34-39: Usable but needs sharpening
- 28-33: Average, not priority
- Below 28: Kill

Automatic penalty:

- Generic idea: max 32/50
- No buyer hesitation: max 35/50
- No creative direction: max 36/50
- Corporate language: max 32/50
- Risky claim: max 30/50
- Attracts wrong customers: max 36/50
- Cannot be tested tomorrow: max 34/50
