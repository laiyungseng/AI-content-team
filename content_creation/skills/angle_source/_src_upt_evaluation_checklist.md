# Evaluation Checklist

Before accepting the output, check:

## Pain and Market

- Are the pain points specific?
- Is market pressure clearly explained?
- Does the output mention why the buyer hesitates?
- Does it identify the wrong-fit customer?
- Does it identify the best-fit customer?

## Angle Quality

- Are the angles meaningfully different?
- Is there strong contrast?
- Does each angle connect to the offer?
- Does each angle have a visual direction?
- Can at least 3 ideas be tested tomorrow?

## Language

- Is the language suitable for Malaysian Chinese market?
- Does it avoid generic corporate phrases?
- Does it sound human?
- Does it avoid translated-English feeling?

## Scoring

- Is scoring strict enough?
- Were weak ideas killed or rewritten?
- Are top 3 ideas genuinely stronger than the others?

## Risk

- Are risky claims flagged?
- For finance, does it avoid guaranteed approval?
- For courses, does it avoid unrealistic outcome promises?

Final pass condition:

The output is only acceptable if at least 3 concepts can realistically become ads tomorrow.
