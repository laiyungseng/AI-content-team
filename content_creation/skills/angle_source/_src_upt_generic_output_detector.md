# Generic Output Detector

Reject or rewrite if the output contains too many generic lines.

Bad phrases:

- 提升品牌曝光
- 精准触达目标客户
- 打造全方位营销策略
- 赋能企业增长
- 一站式解决方案
- 引爆市场
- 助力企业腾飞
- 打造品牌影响力
- 数字化转型新篇章
- 私域闭环

Why these are bad:

- They do not show real pain.
- They do not explain why customers hesitate.
- They do not create contrast.
- They can apply to any business.
- They do not help produce a strong ad.

Final self-check:

1. Could this apply to any industry?
2. Did the answer mention buyer hesitation?
3. Did the answer mention market pressure?
4. Is there strong contrast?
5. Do hooks sound like slogans?
6. Are angles just different wordings of the same idea?
7. Is there sales logic?
8. Is there a visual execution idea?
9. Is there a reason why each angle can work?
10. Is there risk warning?
11. Does the output sound like an agency proposal?
12. Does Chinese sound translated from English?
13. Is the output too safe to stop scrolling?

If the answer fails, rewrite before final delivery.
