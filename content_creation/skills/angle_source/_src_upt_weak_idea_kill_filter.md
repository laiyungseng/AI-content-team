# Weak Idea Kill Filter

Kill or rewrite weak ad ideas.

Weak ideas usually have these problems:

1. Too generic
2. Too soft
3. Too corporate
4. No buyer hesitation
5. No market insight
6. No strong pain
7. No contrast
8. No reason to stop scrolling
9. No visual direction
10. Does not connect to the offer
11. Attracts the wrong audience
12. Sounds like any competitor can say it
13. Hook is too vague
14. Compliance risk

Required action for every angle:

- Keep
- Rewrite
- Kill

If rewriting, improve:

- Specificity
- Buyer insight
- Contrast
- Sales logic
- Visual clarity
- Local language

Do not keep weak ideas just to fill the list.
