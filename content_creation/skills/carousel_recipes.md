# carousel_recipes

Starting sequences by kind of business, so the user is not left to choose from scratch. They are hypotheses: the account's own competitor evidence (what is working in this niche, from the intelligence run) outranks a recipe. Layout names are in [[carousel_generation]].

| Business | Sequence (reason) | Typical layouts |
|---|---|---|
| Local B2C services (car, food, fitness, beauty, medical, wedding, photography, renovation) | Before/after (visible result), testimonials (trust), packages and prices (conversion) | cover, image-caption (before), image-caption (after), stat-cards, cta |
| B2B professional services (finance, legal, accounting, insurance, agency, SaaS, consulting) | Framework or formula (authority), case breakdown (data), mistakes to avoid (high saves) | cover, quote, numbered-step, stat-cards, testimonial, cta |
| Products and e-commerce (fashion, beauty, baby, home, food, D2C) | Customer use photos (real), product vs product (decision help), use scenes (self-projection) | cover, image-caption x3, comparison, cta |
| Education and training | Student testimonials (conversion), framework (what you will learn), mini course (value first) | cover, quote, numbered-step, testimonial x2, cta |
| Personal brand, coach, creator | Quote poster (shares), mistakes to avoid (saves), story or journey (trust) | cover, big-statement x2, numbered-step, cta |
| Property and assets (property, investment, credit, insurance) | Numbers (persuasion), calculator comparison (decision tool), case story (peer reference) | cover, stat-cards x2, comparison, testimonial, cta |

Regulated businesses (finance, investment, health, education outcomes): check every number and testimonial against `shared/platform_rules/meta.md` before it goes on a page.
