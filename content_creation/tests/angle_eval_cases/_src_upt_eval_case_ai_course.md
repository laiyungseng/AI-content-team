# Eval Case: AI Marketing Automation Course

Industry:
AI marketing training

Product/Service:
[brand] AI Marketing Automation course

Target audience:
Marketers, business owners, coaches, SME owners, people who want to use AI to produce marketing work faster

Main problem:
They use ChatGPT casually but cannot build a system. They jump between tools but do not get consistent marketing output.

Offer:
Teach them how to build an AI marketing employee / AI marketing system that can help with research, ads, copywriting, design direction, and workflow

Wrong-fit customers:
People looking for a cheap prompt list, people who only want tools but do not want to build workflow, people expecting AI to magically do everything

Best-fit customers:
Marketers, business owners, coaches, trainers, and SME teams who want to build repeatable marketing workflows with AI

Language:
Malaysian Chinese

Platform:
Facebook, Instagram, XHS, short video

Expected output:

- Must not position it as a normal AI tools class
- Must use "打造系统" or "打造 AI 营销员工"
- Must mention the gap between using AI chat and building an actual workflow/system
- Must generate hooks suitable for course ads
- Must avoid unrealistic income claims
