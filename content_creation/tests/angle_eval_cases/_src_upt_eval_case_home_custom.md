# Eval Case: Home Custom Industry

Industry:
Home renovation, interior design, custom cabinet, furniture/home living

Product/Service:
[brand] marketing service for large home-related businesses

Target audience:
Large home/custom furniture/interior design business owners in Klang Valley with marketing budget

Location:
Malaysia, especially Klang Valley

Main problem:
China sellers entering the market, price war, lead quality dropping, customers comparing prices, ads becoming less effective

Offer:
[brand] helps home industry brands reposition their marketing, attract better quality leads, and avoid competing only on price

Wrong-fit customers:
Small businesses with no budget, businesses only looking for cheap posting service, owners who only want more leads but do not care about lead quality

Best-fit customers:
Established home industry companies with sales team, showroom, project portfolio, and monthly marketing budget

Language:
Malaysian Chinese

Platform:
Facebook, Instagram, short video

Expected output:

- Must mention price war
- Must mention China sellers
- Must mention low-quality leads
- Must mention customer trust
- Must mention why customers compare
- Must produce sharp hooks for business owners
- Must not sound like generic branding advice
