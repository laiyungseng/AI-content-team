# Eval Case: SME Financing

Industry:
SME financing

Product/Service:
Business financing consultation

Target audience:
Malaysia SME owners who need working capital, renovation capital, equipment upgrade, or branch expansion

Main problem:
Need funds but worry about rejection, documents, CTOS/CCRIS, approval, and process

Offer:
Consultation to assess financing options

Wrong-fit customers:
People expecting guaranteed approval, people with no documents, people asking for illegal shortcuts, people expecting instant money without assessment

Best-fit customers:
Malaysia SME owners with SSM, bank statement, business activity, and realistic financing needs

Language:
Malay or Malaysian Chinese

Platform:
Facebook, Instagram

Claims to avoid:
Guaranteed approval, 100% approval, government endorsement, misleading promises, blacklist confirm can get

Expected output:

- Must flag compliance risks
- Must avoid guaranteed approval
- Must use safe wording
- Must still create strong ad angles without illegal or misleading claims
- Must include terms such as subject to approval, assessment, and eligibility where relevant
