# Eval case: AI marketing automation course

- **Industry:** AI marketing training
- **Product/Service:** a course on AI marketing automation
- **Target audience:** marketers, business owners, coaches, SME owners who want AI to produce marketing work faster
- **Main problem:** they use a chatbot casually but cannot build a system; they hop between tools with no consistent output
- **Offer:** teach them to build an "AI marketing employee" that supports research, ads, copy, design direction and workflow
- **Wrong-fit:** people wanting a cheap prompt list or only tools, people unwilling to build a workflow, people expecting AI to do everything
- **Best-fit:** marketers, owners, coaches and SME teams who want repeatable AI workflows
- **Platform:** Facebook, Instagram, XHS, short video

**Must**
- Not position it as a normal AI-tools class; use "build a system" or "AI marketing employee".
- Name the gap between chatting with AI and running a real workflow.
- Give hooks suitable for course ads.
- Avoid unrealistic income claims.
