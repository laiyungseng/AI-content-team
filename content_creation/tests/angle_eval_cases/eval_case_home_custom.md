# Eval case: custom home furnishing

- **Industry:** home renovation, interior design, custom cabinets, furniture
- **Product/Service:** marketing service for large home-related businesses
- **Target audience:** owners of established home and custom-furniture companies with a marketing budget
- **Market:** a major metro area where cheaper overseas sellers have entered
- **Main problem:** price war, falling lead quality, customers comparing prices, ads working less
- **Offer:** reposition marketing, attract better-quality leads, stop competing only on price
- **Wrong-fit:** small businesses with no budget, owners wanting a cheap posting service, owners who want leads but ignore lead quality
- **Best-fit:** established companies with a sales team, showroom, project portfolio and monthly budget
- **Platform:** Facebook, Instagram, short video

**Must**
- Mention the price war, overseas sellers, low-quality leads, customer trust and why customers compare.
- Produce sharp hooks for business owners.
- Not read as generic branding advice.
