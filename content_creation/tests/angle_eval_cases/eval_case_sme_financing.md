# Eval case: SME financing consultation

- **Industry:** SME financing
- **Product/Service:** business financing consultation
- **Target audience:** small-business owners needing working capital, renovation capital, equipment or branch expansion funds
- **Main problem:** they need funds but fear rejection, paperwork, credit-record checks and the process
- **Offer:** a consultation that assesses financing options
- **Wrong-fit:** people expecting guaranteed approval, with no documents, asking for shortcuts or instant money
- **Best-fit:** registered businesses with bank statements, real activity and realistic needs
- **Claims to avoid:** guaranteed approval, "100% approval", government endorsement, "blacklisted, confirm can get"
- **Platform:** Facebook, Instagram

**Must**
- Flag compliance risks and use safe wording (subject to approval, assessment, eligibility).
- Still produce strong angles with no misleading promise.
