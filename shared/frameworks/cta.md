# Calls to Action

From a business owner's point of view the purpose of content is to convert views into leads and sales, not to collect views and followers. Every video needs a CTA to bridge content to conversion.

This is a lookup file. When writing a script, read section 4 to pick the type and section 2 for how to place it. Read the whole thing when the diagnosis is "views but no leads".

## Contents
1. Why every video gets one

## 1. Why every video gets one

Run a CTA on every single video, without exception. Not because every video will convert, but because a CTA is the only way to measure which videos actually drive leads and sales. Without it there is no signal to double down on.

## 2. Embedding CTAs naturally

The best CTAs do not feel like an ad. Two reliable ways to embed one:

**Extended value CTA.** After delivering a framework or insight, offer a related free resource as the natural extension of what was just taught.

> "By the way, if you want that full process I just walked through in an easy to fill out template, I put a guide together for you. Comment [word] and you'll get it."

**Product as answer CTA.** Reference the product or tool as if it is simply the next step in the content, not a pitch.

> "First we go to [tool], then I do X, Y and Z."

Place the CTA where it fits the natural flow of the story, not wherever is most convenient. Never break the flow of the story to insert it. If the only place it fits is the very end, that is fine, but check first whether it lands better mid-script.

## 3. The three categories and ten types

Ranked from highest to lowest converting within each category.

### Follow CTAs

Use only when there is no lead magnet to offer. Followers alone do not convert to revenue.

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Value promise | Position yourself as an ongoing source of value | "I teach [topic], follow for more industry education." |
| Transformation story | Use a before and after journey to build credibility | "I went from X pain to Y result. If you want the same, follow for more." |
| Avatar targeted | Call out a specific audience directly | "If you're a business owner looking for [result], follow for more." |

### Engagement CTAs

Generally low priority. Viewers engage naturally when the content is genuinely good.

| Type | Mechanism | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Save | "Save this for later" | Only works if the content is genuinely worth saving |
| Tag someone | Prompt a tag | Reads as engagement farming, generally avoid |
| Comment / opinion | Invite a take | Only works with genuinely debate-worthy content |

### Lead CTAs

The highest priority category. These drive email capture and business leads directly.

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet offer | Trade a free resource for a comment or DM | "DM me the word [X] for a free [resource]." |
| Comment for info | Lower friction, open-ended exchange | "If you want more info, comment [X] and I'll send it over." |
| Transformation and trigger | Before and after story plus a comment CTA | "I went from X to Y. Want the same results? Comment for details." |
| Direct pitch | Pitch the paid offer directly | Use sparingly, reads as the most sales-heavy option |

A comment-based lead magnet normally triggers an automation flow that delivers the resource and captures the email. Suggest the comment trigger word as part of the script, not as an afterthought.

## 4. Priority order

When writing a CTA into a script, work down this list and stop at the first one available:

1. A customised lead magnet mapped directly to this video's topic
2. A general call for information ("comment X for more details")
3. A transformation story paired with a comment CTA
4. Direct selling (avoid using often)
5. A follow CTA, only if none of the above exist

If the user has no lead magnet at all, that is worth naming as the real constraint rather than defaulting quietly to a follow CTA. A follow CTA is the fallback, not the answer.

## 5. Written-copy CTA block
For long-form post and ad copy the CTA is a block, not a line: urgency, specific offer, contact channels (primary chat link first, then URL, phone, address for physical services), then hashtags. Scarcity and CTA are both required, never one or the other. A button line carries no icon emoji. Details and hashtag mix: `content_creation/skills/ad_copy_longform.md`.
