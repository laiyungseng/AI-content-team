# Experimentation

## Allocation (strategy mix)
| Share | Bucket | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 70% | Proven | Patterns validated in `memory/[project]/winning_patterns.md` |
| 20% | Adjacent | A proven pattern applied to a new topic, format or platform |
| 10% | Experimental | Untested idea from an insight or competitor gap |

Intent mix (Educate / Engage / Promote) is a separate axis; every planned post gets both tags. See [[storytelling]].

## Running an experiment
1. State a hypothesis in one line: "If we change X, then Y improves, because Z."
2. Change **one** variable (hook, format, length, CTA, posting time). Keep the rest comparable.
3. Define the metric and the baseline before publishing (ratio to our own typical, see [[stats_rules]]).
4. Publish at least 3 posts per variant when possible; a single post is an anecdote.
5. Record in `memory/[project]/experiments.md`: hypothesis, posts (count), result, confidence (low/medium/high).

## Promoting a result
- A finding moves to `winning_patterns.md` or `failed_patterns.md` only with evidence: post count, our own validation, result, confidence.
- Results that fail the "too early to call" rule stay in `experiments.md`.
- Repeated user overrides or tool problems go to `memory/_system/`, not brand memory.
