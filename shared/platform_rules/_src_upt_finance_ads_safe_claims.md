# Finance Ads Safe Claims

For financing, loans, grants, SME funding, working capital, CTOS, CCRIS, approval, or blacklist topics, avoid risky claims.

Do not use:

- Guaranteed approval
- 100% approve
- No rejection
- Blacklist also confirm can get
- Government-backed claim unless verified
- Instant money
- No document needed
- Sure pass
- Misleading association with official bodies
- Overpromising speed or outcome

Use safer phrasing:

- Subject to approval
- Terms and conditions apply
- Eligibility depends on documents and assessment
- May be suitable for qualified businesses
- Speak to consultant for assessment
- 先评估文件和资格，再看适合哪一种方案
- 不是乱申请，而是先知道自己适合什么

Finance angle direction:

- Keep urgency, but move away from guaranteed outcome.
- Speak to fear of rejection, confusion, documents, and wrong application.
- Make assessment the bridge to action.
- Filter out people expecting illegal shortcuts or instant approval.
