# Meta Ads Safe Claims

Keep ad claims specific but safe.

Avoid:

- Personal attribute assumptions such as "Are you in debt?" or "You are blacklisted."
- Guaranteed income, approval, medical, or education results.
- Before/after claims that imply every customer will get the same result.
- Aggressive fear language that attacks a protected or sensitive status.

Safer patterns:

- Speak to business situations, not personal identity.
- Use "many owners face..." instead of "you are..."
- Use "may help", "designed for", "assessment", and "case example" where needed.
- Keep proof factual and avoid stretching one case into a universal claim.
