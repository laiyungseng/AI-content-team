# Sensitive Claims

Flag risky claims, especially for:

- Financing
- Loans
- Investment
- Income claims
- Health
- Beauty
- Education outcome
- Government-related claims
- Approval claims
- Guaranteed results
- Before/after transformations that imply guaranteed outcome

Avoid:

- Guaranteed outcomes
- Unrealistic speed promises
- Misleading government association
- Medical, income, approval, or investment claims without proof
- Claims that imply every user will get the same result

Safer wording patterns:

- Results depend on documents, execution, market, budget, and assessment.
- Terms and conditions apply.
- Suitability depends on the customer situation.
- Use assessment, diagnosis, or consultation language where appropriate.
- Show proof as examples, not guaranteed outcomes.
